| **Lükas? no — FT/The Economist** (4.2M) | Institutional visual journalism | Ruthless information density, thin-rule Swiss layouts |

**Patterns that win in this niche (and what we take from them):**

1. **One question per video**, answered completely by the end ("Why is everything more expensive?"). — Take from Economics Explained.
2. **Charts ARE the B-roll.** Animated line/bar charts with a cited source chip in the corner beat generic stock footage for trust. — Take from Economics Explained + The Economist.
3. **Every claim gets a receipt.** Source chip on screen (BLS, Eurostat, ECB, FRED). Builds authority; also protects us when numbers are contested. — Take from all three.
4. **A human POV, even if faceless** — a narrator with a point of view, not a Wikipedia voice. — Take from Garys Economics / Unlearning Economics.
5. **Real examples first, theory second.** A receipt, a rent screenshot, a fuel price board — then the concept, never the reverse. — Take from Two Cents.
6. **Episode length 6–12 min**; our 8:00 target is squarely in the sweet spot.
7. **Thumbnail = one huge number/word + one icon + ≤4 words**, consistent palette. Nobody in ES-language economics runs a tight thumbnail system — open lane.

**Language lane decision (affects everything):** economics in **Spanish** is underserved at premium quality (VisualPolitik is geopolitics-first; Rallo is talk-first, visuals-second). **English** is crowded but bigger. **Bilingual** doubles script work but builds a bridge audience. → Your call (see questions).

## 2. Positioning statement (working)

> An economics channel that explains the world with today's numbers — one real example, one question, zero jargon.

Cadence target: 1 episode/week to start; pillar mix ≈ 60% explainers (current events) · 25% concept-with-cases ("How X actually works") · 15% "the number that matters" quickies/shorts.

## 3. Three brand directions

### Option A — “The Visible Economy” / “La Economía Visible”
**Tagline:** "The economy, made visible." · "La economía, a simple vista."
**Look:** editorial print DNA (Financial Times restraint + PolyMatter warmth). Warm paper background `#F2EDE3`, near-black ink `#14181F`, one signal accent — **vermilion** `#E8480C`, secondary deep teal `#0F6F6C`.
**Type:** Anton (display/thumbnails, we already vendor it) + Archivo (body) + tabular numerals everywhere.
**Logo:** circle containing an eye whose pupil is a rising line-chart — "seeing the invisible hand". Works at 48px avatar.
**Thumbnail formula:** paper card, giant Anton headline (≤4 words), one vermilion data-shape (arrow/chart/percent), micro source line bottom-left.
**Sting:** 2.5s — single piano note + paper rustle + low thud. No choir, no whoosh storm.
**Personality:** calm authority; the friend who reads The Economist for you.

### Option B — “El Precio de Todo” / “The Price of Everything”
**Tagline:** "Todo tiene un precio — y una historia." · "Everything has a price — and a story."
**Look:** warm chalkboard ledger. Deep green board `#10312B`, cream chalk `#EDE6D3`, **chalk yellow** `#FFD84D` accent, coral chalk `#FF6B57` secondary. Faint ledger ruled lines at 4% opacity; hand-drawn circles/underlines (we already have the marker-draw technique from the pipeline).
**Type:** Anton + Archivo, chalk fills slightly textured; numbers always in chalk yellow.
**Logo:** hand-drawn chalk coin with €/$ and a small crack splitting it (inflation!)
**Thumbnail formula:** big chalk price tag + ≤4 words + one hand-drawn arrow/circle; yellow highlight slab behind key word.
**Sting:** 2.5s — chalk tap, page flip, soft rim-shot. Classroom warmth without cheese.
**Personality:** your favourite economics teacher; every video starts with something you can buy, owe or earn.

### Option C — “Línea Base” / “Baseline”
**Tagline:** "Economía sin humo." · "Economics, no smoke."
**Look:** Swiss data-journalism — dark slate `#0E1116`, off-white `#F2F2EE`, **signal coral** `#FF5A47` accent; 1px hairline rules, generous margins, huge tabular numerals, faint graph-paper grid.
**Type:** Anton + Archivo with strict 8px baseline grid; thin Archivo for captions/sources.
**Logo:** dotted horizontal baseline with a bold coral line breaking through it. Swiss, cold, precise.
**Thumbnail formula:** dark card, enormous coral numeral or 3-word black-on-white slab; thin rule + source caption.
**Sting:** 1.5s — single tuned tick + sub-bass drop. Newsroom confidence.
**Personality:** data newsroom for people who hate hype; the inbox header you trust.

**My recommendation:** **Option B if the channel is Spanish-first** (open lane, highest warmth, strongest fit for "real examples, today's bills"), **Option A if English-first or bilingual** (most credible at scale, easiest to extend beyond ES). Option C is the strongest for a newsroom tone if you prefer harder, numbers-only energy.
